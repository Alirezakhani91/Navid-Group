HERO
A little about Navid.
Navid works in accounting and runs two businesses based in Saint John: PCB&T and NAV’s Property Management. His background includes work in the automotive, energy, aquaculture and property sectors.

01 / BACKGROUND
A bit of background.
Navid has spent much of his working life around accounting, financial records and the systems businesses use to manage them.
He has worked in several different industries, including automotive, energy, aquaculture and property operations. Each has its own procedures, deadlines and practical issues to deal with.
Today, he runs PCB&T and NAV’s Property Management in Saint John. One focuses on accounting and tax services; the other helps with the day-to-day responsibilities of rental properties.

02 / THE BUSINESSES
Two businesses in Saint John.
PCB&T and NAV’s serve different needs. Here’s a brief look at what each business does.

NAV’s Property Management
Rental property management.
NAV’s Property Management works with property owners on rental administration and everyday property matters. Depending on the arrangement, that can include tenant communication, coordinating repairs and maintenance, and keeping track of rent-related details.

PCB&T
Bookkeeping and tax services.
PCB&T offers bookkeeping, bank reconciliations, payroll support, financial reporting, and business and personal tax services. Some clients need help with an ongoing routine; others have a specific task or question.

04 / GETTING IN TOUCH
What can you ask us about?
Bookkeeping and records: Maybe the books have fallen behind, or you're looking for someone to handle regular bookkeeping. PCB&T can discuss what records you have and what support you need.
Personal or business taxes: If you have questions about a return or are preparing documents for tax time, you can reach out to PCB&T to talk about the situation.
A rental property: For questions about tenant communication, maintenance or rental administration, NAV’s is the place to start.
