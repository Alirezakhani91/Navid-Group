# About Navid — V5 content revision

This version revises the English copy throughout `about-navid.html`. It intentionally uses everyday, modest language and stays focused on verifiable background and available services.

## Approach
- No dramatic taglines, third-party praise, made-up testimonials or exaggerated claims.
- Experiences are presented as a straightforward list, not evidence of exceptional performance.
- Service descriptions are specific but do not promise results.
- No personal quotes were invented or attributed to Navid.
- Only 'Navid' is used in the on-page copy.

## Facts to confirm with Navid before publication
- Names of previous workplaces and the nature of his experience with each.
- BBA degree title and concentration at UNB Saint John, including 2015 date.
- CompuCollege qualification/date (2008) and Mohawk College QuickBooks training/date (2022).
- Exact scope of accounting, tax and property-management services.
- Business contact addresses and phone number.

## Publish on existing Navid Group GitHub Pages
To change **only the About page**, upload `about-navid.html` from the patch ZIP to the same directory as `index.html`. No image, stylesheet, homepage or script replacement is required. For a clean repository, the complete ZIP contains all project files.
